FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Every new account gets a full 7-day free trial — no credit card required to start. You'll have access to all features in your chosen plan. At the end of the trial you can subscribe or let it expire. We'll send a reminder 3 days before your trial ends.

We currently support Wegmans, GIANT, ALDI, Trader Joe's, and Weis Markets in the State College area. Walmart pricing is coming soon. We're actively working to expand coverage — if there's a store you'd like to see added, let us know on our Contact page.

Crowdsourced prices are submitted by real shoppers in your area and are typically very accurate for the week they're submitted. Our system flags outliers and cross-references submissions against store API data where available. Prices are refreshed every week when the bingo board resets, so data stays current. As always, we recommend verifying the price in-store before making a purchase decision.

Yes — absolutely. There are no contracts or cancellation fees. You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. You'll retain access until the end of your current billing period, and you won't be charged again after that.

Each week we publish a 10×10 bingo board with 100 grocery products across 5 local stores. Submit the real price for each item (with an optional shelf tag photo) to fill your board. Complete all 100 in a single week and you earn 5% off your next monthly subscription. The discount stacks each week you complete — hit all 4 weeks in a month and you earn the maximum 20% off. The board resets every Monday.

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover) through our secure payment processor, Stripe. Apple Pay and Google Pay are also supported on compatible devices. We do not store your card details — all payment information is handled directly by Stripe.
